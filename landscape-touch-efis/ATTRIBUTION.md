## Unverified figures

- Native resolution 1280 x 768 pixels (5:3) comes from a manufacturer announcement quoted on a public
  forum, not from the installation manual's text.
- The outer bezel (272 x 195 mm), the control positions along the bottom row, and the 60 percent PFD
  width in split mode are the engine's own proportions.
