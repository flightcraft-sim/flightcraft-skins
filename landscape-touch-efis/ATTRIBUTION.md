documents. No text, image, icon, or code was copied, and nothing was traced; every instrument is drawn

## How the placement figures were obtained

The pilot's guide's figures give arrangement, not dimensions. The engine's geometry for the full-screen
and split-screen primary flight display was measured from the p. 51 figure and the p. 19 and p. 20
split-mode figures, scaled to the model's 1280 x 768 pixels. Each number is good to a few percent.

## Unverified figures

- Native resolution 1280 x 768 pixels (5:3) comes from a manufacturer announcement quoted on a public
  forum, not from the installation manual's text.
- The outer bezel (272 x 195 mm) and the control positions along the bottom row are the engine's own
  proportions.
- The map range ladder: the guide states 23 ranges and that their distances depend on configuration; it
  prints the steps 2, 3, 5, 8, 12, 20, 30, 50, 80 and 120 in its map setup lists. The other steps in the
  engine follow the same progression and are not from the guide.
- Pixel sizes of text, line weights and the pitch scale are the engine's own.
